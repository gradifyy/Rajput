# Before an official launch

Confirm the business name, street address/floor, logo, public telephone, opening hours and closure days. Supply the current menu with prices, accurate descriptions, allergen information and any supported dietary classifications. Approve Korean/English copy, photographs and their usage rights.

Confirm takeaway/delivery availability, parking/accessibility and any reservation workflow before adding those claims or actions. No payment, booking or ordering backend is included in this preview.

Approve the final domain and public launch. Download and scan the QR from that final public destination before printing customer cards. Keep preview indexing disabled until approval. Publishing source to a repository does not itself launch the restaurant site or approve its content.
