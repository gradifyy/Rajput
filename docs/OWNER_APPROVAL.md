# Owner approval before official launch

This is a reviewable demonstration. Nothing here grants official restaurant launch or table-card distribution approval. Existing preview publication and repository development do not imply endorsement by Rajput. No restaurant contact or purchase has been made.

- [ ] Confirm Korean and English business names; approve or replace the proposed wordmark/favicon with licensed official artwork.
- [ ] Confirm the road address, customer entrance, floor, landmark and exact Naver/Kakao/Google place links.
- [ ] Resolve three conflicting phone leads and approve the current contact method.
- [ ] Confirm daily hours, last order, closing days and holiday exceptions.
- [ ] Supply the complete current menu with approved Korean/English names, descriptions, portions, prices/currency, availability and category order.
- [ ] Specifically review tandoori half/whole, plain naan and the questionable legacy listing; confirm whether excluded paneer/papad or historical sets should return.
- [ ] Confirm ingredients, allergens, spice and dietary information before adding any labels.
- [ ] Supply licensed real food and interior photography; approve crops/alt text and replace all generated conceptual images.
- [ ] Confirm reservation, takeaway, accessibility, parking, pets and other customer policies before adding claims or actions.
- [ ] Approve final Korean/English copy and proposed visual identity.
- [ ] Approve final public domain, deployment and search indexing separately from this unofficial demo.
- [ ] From that final public origin, download/print the QR card, check paper size and contrast, and scan with a real phone while signed out. Confirm it reaches `/menu/` without an account, then test filters, search, language and the home link.
- [ ] Only after these checks, remove the demo mark and distribute table cards. Keep the QR destination stable through future deployments.

The current implementation uses no payment, account, booking, analytics, or customer-data backend. Introducing any such service needs a separate agreed scope and appropriate real configuration.
