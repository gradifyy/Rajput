## Images
The local stock photographs are from Unsplash; exact source URLs and intended usage are recorded in `src/data.js`. License: https://unsplash.com/license . These are conceptual photographs, not authentic photographs of Rajput dishes or premises. No review-site images have been reused. Replace with owner-approved photographs before an official launch. The flatbread image is a crop for visual mood, not a claim about the restaurant's exact naan.

## Typography
Cormorant Garamond, Manrope and Noto Sans KR are requested from Google Fonts. Local system fonts remain the fallback when fonts cannot load. Font delivery is the only external resource request made on initial page load; photographs, icons and QR generation are local.
